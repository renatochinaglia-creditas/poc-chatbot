# QuantumHound Time-Travel Policies & Guidelines

Welcome to QuantumHound, the premier provider of immersive time-travel experiences. To ensure a safe and enjoyable journey through time, all travelers must adhere to the following policies and guidelines.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Travelers must be **18 years or older** or accompanied by a legal guardian.
- A **comprehensive health screening** is required prior to travel to ensure fitness for temporal displacement.
- All travelers must complete and sign the **QuantumHound Temporal Liability Waiver** before departure.
- Certain medical conditions, including but not limited to **unstable quantum resonance disorders (UQRD)** and **temporal displacement syndrome (TDS)**, may disqualify individuals from travel.
- All bookings are processed in USD. Historical or future currencies listed in the Travel Package Catalog are for use during travel only.

## Approved Destinations

- Travelers may only select from pre-approved destinations listed in the official QuantumHound Travel Package Catalog.
- **Unauthorized historical events, high-risk periods, and paradox-prone locations** are not available for travel.
- Requests for unlisted time periods, locations, or speculative futures **will not be accommodated** for safety, ethical, and timeline integrity reasons.

## Safety Guidelines

- **DO NOT INTERFERE WITH HISTORICAL EVENTS.** Even minor alterations may have cascading effects on the timeline.
- Travelers must wear their **QuantumHound ChronoBadge** at all times to ensure synchronization with return protocols.
- **Do not share anachronistic knowledge** (e.g., stock market trends, technological advances) with historical figures.
- **Avoid direct interactions with high-profile historical figures** unless explicitly permitted in the travel package.
- In the event of temporal drift, remain calm and activate your **Emergency Recall Beacon (ERB)**.
- Travelers are responsible for following their assigned **Temporal Guide's** instructions at all times.

## Packing Recommendations

- **Prohibited Items:** Modern currency, electronic devices, anachronistic books or literature, weapons (unless part of an approved excursion).
- **Recommended Items:** Clothing appropriate to the destination period (provided upon booking), a QuantumHound **emergency translation device**, and a personalized itinerary.
- All personal belongings must be registered with QuantumHound before travel.

## Liability & Disclaimers

- QuantumHound is **not responsible for paradoxes** resulting from traveler actions that violate guidelines.
- While QuantumHound's time-travel technology is **99.997% accurate**, minor temporal shifts may occur.
- Travelers **assume all responsibility** for their experiences and interactions in the past and future.
- QuantumHound reserves the right to **terminate travel privileges** for any traveler engaging in reckless behavior.

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policies

- **Cancellations made within 48 hours of departure** are non-refundable due to quantum stabilization costs.
- Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least **one week prior to the scheduled departure date**.
- In the event of **temporal turbulence**, QuantumHound reserves the right to reschedule trips at no additional charge.

For further inquiries, please contact your assigned **Temporal Concierge**. Thank you for choosing QuantumHound—where the past, present, and future are just a leap away!
