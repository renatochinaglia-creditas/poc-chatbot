**QuantumHound Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

---

## Can I choose any time period or historical event for travel?
No. QuantumHound travel packages are limited to **pre-approved destinations** listed in the official Travel Package Catalog. Custom destinations or speculative time periods are not supported.

---

## Can I request to meet a specific historical figure not listed in the catalog?
No. All interactions are limited to those described in the Travel Package Catalog. Private audiences, when offered, are part of select Premium or Deluxe Tier packages only.

---

## Can I pay in historical currencies like francs, denarii, or gold dust?
No. All purchases must be made in **USD** at the time of booking. Historical currencies listed in the Travel Package Catalog are only used during the time-travel experience itself.

---

## Do I need to bring anything to the event?
No. QuantumHound provides all necessary equipment, including safety gear, communication aids, and era-appropriate clothing.

---

## What happens if I try to change history?
Attempting to alter known events, reveal future knowledge, or interfere with major historical outcomes is strictly forbidden. Violations may result in immediate recall, loss of travel privileges, and possible legal or temporal enforcement.

---

## Is it dangerous to travel to the past?
There are inherent risks to all time travel. QuantumHound minimizes them through safety protocols, local stabilization technology, and on-site Temporal Guides. Travelers assume responsibility as outlined in the Liability Summary.

---

## What is the ChronoBadge?
The QuantumHound ChronoBadge is a wearable device that ensures safe reentry, provides translation, and serves as your identity during time-travel excursions.

---

## Can I extend my trip or visit multiple destinations?
Only travelers with an **Ultimate ChronoPass** package may request extended or multi-destination itineraries. All extensions are subject to timeline stability review.

---

## What happens if I get separated from the group?
Your ChronoBadge includes a Recall Beacon and GPS tracker linked to the local Guide. Stay where you are and wait for a retrieval team.

---

## Is there a refund policy?
Sales are final unless otherwise specified in the event listing. Exceptions may be made for mechanical or temporal disruptions under QuantumHound's Traveler Policy.

---

## What is the policy for trip cancellation?
Cancellations made within 48 hours of departure are non-refundable. If you need to cancel, please contact QuantumHound support prior to 48 hours before your scheduled departure time to discuss options.

---
