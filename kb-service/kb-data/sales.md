QuantumHound Travel Sales Overview
==================================

QuantumHound offers strictly curated travel experiences to predefined historical and future events. All available destinations are listed in the official **QuantumHound Travel Package Catalog**.

> ⚠️ **No custom time travel periods, destinations, or events are available.** Travelers may only purchase pre-configured packages from the catalog.

---

Pricing Tiers
-------------

Travel experiences are offered in tiered packages. Each tier reflects the level of access and exclusivity:

### • Standard Tier
Includes general admission or base-level access to an event.
- Example: Festival Entry at Pharaoh Hatshepsut's Regatta — 2 Debens of Copper
- Example: Reading Admission at Howl Premiere — 1 Dollar

### • Premium Tier
Includes immersive participation or exclusive vantage points.
- Example: Royal Barge Access at the Nile Regatta — 10 Debens of Silver
- Example: Balcony Seating at *Hamlet* Premiere — 5 Shillings

### • Deluxe Tier
Includes limited-access or high-risk premium experiences.
- Example: Thought-Relay Panel Access at Singularity Summit — 100 Data Credits
- Example: Private Audience with da Vinci — 25 Florins

All package pricing is specified using the **local era currency**, with an estimated value in USD available in the Travel Package Catalog's `exchange_rate` field.

---

Payment and Booking
-------------------

Full payment is required **at the time of booking**, and all payments to QuantumHound are made in **USD**. The historical and future currencies listed in the Travel Package Catalog are for use on-site during your time-travel experience.
- All bookings are charged in USD. Local era currencies listed in the Travel Package Catalog are for use during the time-travel experience itself.
- All sales are final unless explicitly noted otherwise in the individual package listing.
- Bookings are made exclusively through the QuantumHound platform.

> For up-to-date pricing, package descriptions, and exchange rate guidance, refer to the **QuantumHound Travel Package Catalog**.

> A currency converter tool will be available soon to help travelers preview historical pricing in modern USD.

---

For safety guidelines, time-travel eligibility, and refund policies, consult the Traveler Policies document included in your onboarding packet.
